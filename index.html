Typing defense

Soulward

Type each falling word before it breaks your ward.

Every word you type becomes a stone in your wall, and a typo breaks the newest one. Every fifteen words a boss arrives with its own attack, such as darkness that comes in waves, mirrored words or frozen letters. You survive its attack, then strike its weak point.

Modes
Pilgrim, Knight and Ashen paths, each with its own best score
Input
On-screen keys or your device keyboard
Language
English and Bahasa Melayu
Extras
Bosses, word codex, event mode with a leaderboard

Arabic word game

Fatil فتيل

Type an Arabic word that contains the two letters before the fuse burns out.

Each round shows a pair of letters on the bomb. Find a real Arabic word that contains them, and the fuse resets for the next pair. A hint ghosts a word onto the keyboard when you are stuck. Every word you find is kept in the prompt book.

Play
Letter pairs, a burning fuse, three lives
Input
Built-in Arabic keyboard or your own
Language
Interface in English or Arabic; words are always Arabic
Extras
Hints, prompt book, event mode with a leaderboard

Shared ecosystem

Tidekin

A tide pool you keep with your friends.

Crabs, snails and fish live across the crevice, the shallows and the surf, and the tide keeps moving. Everyone tends the same pool online: hatch eggs, scatter food and look at each animal's genes. The pool keeps running while you are away, and a shared leaderboard tracks who raised what.

Play
Chance-driven life sim, played together
Players
One shared pool for everyone with the link
Time
Day and night cycle with rising and falling tides
Extras
Genes, rare animals, pool log, leaderboard
